# Monday guide: driver sheets in 5 minutes

For TABLE staff. Print this page and keep it by the computer.

## Every week

1. **Export the week's file** the way you do now (Routific export, or the Monday spreadsheet).
2. **Open the Route Sheets app** (bookmark it in the browser you always use; your building pins live in that browser).
3. **Drag the file onto the page**, or click **Choose a file**.
   - If the columns are the same as last week, you go straight to Review.
   - If you see "Check that we read your file correctly", make sure *Route number* and *Street address* are right, then click **Use these columns**.
4. **Look at the top of the Review page.**
   - Green "Everything is ready": nothing to do. Go to step 5.
   - Yellow "Needs attention": click the button next to each item. See "When something needs attention" below.
5. Click **Continue to print & send**.
6. Click **Print driver sheets**. Each route prints on its own page(s).
7. To text a driver, click **Copy link** next to their route and paste it into the text message. **Copy all links** copies every route at once.
8. If you see "You changed … since your last backup", click **Download backup** and save the file to the shared drive.

## When something needs attention

**"… complexes need building locations"**: a new apartment complex showed up. Click **Set up buildings** (about 5 minutes, once per complex):

1. The satellite map opens on the complex. Click each building's outline (or click **Drop a pin**, then the building).
2. Type each building's name the way drivers will see it ("Building C", "400").
3. Drag each label to where drivers should park, near the front door.
4. Optional: add a note like "Enter from Sycamore Ln side" or "Gate code at office".
5. Under **Say which units are in each building**, add rules, for example:
   - *A range of units*: 101 to 124 → Building 1
   - *Units that start with*: 2 → Building 2 (201, 215, 2B…)
   - *Units that end with*: B → Building B (4B, 11B…)
   - *Units that look like*: 1xx → Building 1 (x means any digit)
   - *One unit*: 7 → Office
6. Check the list under **Check this week's units**. Orange units have no building yet: click one to place it.
7. Click **Save complex**.

If the complex has more than one street address (402, 404 Sycamore…), tick them under "Also part of this complex?".

**"… new units have no building"**: a family moved into a unit your rules don't cover. Click **Add units to buildings**, click the orange unit, pick its building, then **Save complex**. Until you do, that family's stop goes to the complex's main address.

**"… rows were left out"**: those rows had no route number or no address. Fix them in Salesforce/Routific if needed.

**Hard-to-find house?** On the Review page, click **Pin exact spot** under the address, click the driveway on the map, then **Save pin**.

**Not really an apartment?** (for example, a duplex flagged because two families share a street address): click **Not an apartment? Pin this house** under the address.

## Good to know

- Family names, phone numbers and unit numbers never leave this computer.
- Building pins are saved **in this browser on this computer**. Use **Menu (☰) → Download a backup** after changes. On a new computer, use **Menu → Restore from a backup**.
- Drivers need nothing installed. The QR code opens Google Maps on their phone. Long routes have Part 1, Part 2…: scan the next part when one finishes.
- If drivers open links in a phone browser without the Google Maps app, only 4 stops work per link. Set **Print & send → Settings → Stops per link** to 4 for those drivers.
